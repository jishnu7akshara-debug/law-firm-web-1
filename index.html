How a document moves through our desk.

Every property or company document follows the same six steps, and you can see which one your file is on at any time. Keep scrolling to follow a sale deed from first call to registered copy.

1

Brief

A 30-minute call or office visit. We listen, list the papers we need and quote a fixed fee before any work begins.

  • Checklist sent the same day
  • Fee agreed in writing
2

Due diligence

Title search, encumbrance certificate, approvals and a 30-year ownership chain, with every gap written up.

3

Drafting

Clause by clause, in English and Kannada or Telugu where the registrar needs it. You get a marked-up draft, not a template.

  • Two revision rounds included
  • Call to walk through each clause
4

Stamping

We calculate stamp duty and registration fees exactly, buy the e-stamp paper and prepare the final engrossed copy.

  • No surprise government charges
  • Duty receipts shared with you
5

Registration

We book the sub-registrar slot and a lawyer goes with you for signatures, photographs and biometrics.

6

Handover and archive

Original, certified copies and a scanned set in your private folder. We keep a copy on file for twelve years.

  • Mutation and khata follow-up
  • Reminder for renewals and leases
